# Geometric series

What about this sequence?

`1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + 1/16 + 1/32 + .... (going on forever)`

## Zeno's series

This series was discussed by Zeno, an ancient Greek mathematician living in 400 BC. He said:

1. Suppose you have a two metre distance you want to travel
2. You can break that up into steps by travelling half the remaining distance with each step
3. So the first step is 1 metre, and there is one metre left
4. Then the next step is 50 centimeters, and there is 50 centimeters left
5. And the next step is 25 centimeters...
6. This way it takes you infinite (!) steps to cross the distance, and the total distance you cross is 2 meters

This is the same as our sequence - 1 (meter) + 1/2 (metre)
